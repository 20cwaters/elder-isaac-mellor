How a mission works

What is the MTC?

The Missionary Training Center. New missionaries spend their first weeks there learning how to teach before heading out to their actual mission. Isaac started at the one in Provo, Utah, and since his mission is English-speaking, there is no new language to learn.

What is a transfer?

Roughly every six weeks the mission president reassigns missionaries. Isaac may stay put or be moved to a new town, and he may get a new companion either way. Transfer news usually shows up in the weekly email, and the Where He Is page gets updated when it does.

What is a companion?

Missionaries are always paired up. Companions live, study, and teach together for the length of a transfer or longer. You will see a lot of names come and go over the course of a mission.

What is a P-day?

Preparation day — one day a week for laundry, groceries, sightseeing, and writing home. That is when the weekly email arrives, and when he is allowed to call or video chat with his family.
